# Tour

Guided help. Two kinds, one component family under
`src/components/UX/Tour/`.

| Kind | Job |
|---|---|
| Registration | Step-driven onboarding for sign-up. Immersive, skippable, accurate. |
| Interface | On-demand inspector for chrome the user has not found yet. Nudges, not a blocking walkthrough. |

Founder intent for tone and interaction: `src/components/UX/Tour/vision.md`.

Copy follows `VOICE.md`. Do not invent a third tour type or a parallel overlay
system.
