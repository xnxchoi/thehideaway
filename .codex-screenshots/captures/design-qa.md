design-system alignment:
- `readmes/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` now includes the Formal Artifact / Receipt style family.
- The invoice follows that family: centered brand ritual, wide margins, sparse unruled tables, restrained legal copy, and minimal page numerals.
- The continuation approach avoids dashboard-style row chrome; blank rows remain invisible until edited.
- Accepted rule: preallocated empty rows belong only to manually fillable
  templates. Finalized/generated invoices must remove unused rows and pull
  totals, terms, payment details, and footer-adjacent content upward.

known constraints:
- AcroForm PDFs cannot insert unlimited viewer-independent rows. This template supports appendability through preallocated editable rows and continuation pages. Truly unbounded invoices should be generated by the app-side pdfmake renderer.
- Editable AcroForm field text uses PDF-safe form fonts. Static chrome, labels, legal copy, header, footer, page size, margins, and layout remain from the canonical pdfmake render.
- Korean fillable fields are limited to Latin/numeric-safe values unless a Unicode-capable AcroForm font strategy is added. Static Korean labels and copy remain rendered through the canonical font stack.
